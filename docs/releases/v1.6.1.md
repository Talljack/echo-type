# EchoType v1.6.1

## 中文

- 发音学习改用真人录音：48 个音标示范和 107 段英式例词，可离线播放；统一音量、修剪首尾静音。
- 优化播放响应：预加载当前音标，重播复用音频；准确区分“正在加载”和“正在播放”。
- 新增音频来源与署名页面；明确标注辅音示范中的元音环境和例词录音中的冠词。

Desktop：macOS Apple Silicon、Windows x64、Linux x64。保留现有学习数据和设置；不包含 iOS 安装包。

## English

- Use human pronunciation recordings: 48 phonetic demonstrations and 107 British word clips, bundled for offline playback with normalized gain and trimmed leading/trailing silence.
- Preload the selected sound and reuse media on replay; distinguish loading from playing.
- Add source/license credits and disclose supporting vowels and articles retained in recordings.

Desktop: Apple Silicon macOS, Windows x64, and Linux x64. Existing learning data and settings are retained; no iOS package is included.
